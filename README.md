# Business Insights 360 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is an interactive **Power BI business intelligence dashboard** designed to provide a 360° view of business performance across key functional areas.

The dashboard transforms business data into actionable insights for **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive Management**, helping stakeholders track KPIs, identify trends, compare performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to create a centralized reporting solution that enables business users to:

* Monitor overall business performance
* Analyze revenue, sales, margins, and profitability
* Compare actual performance with benchmarks and previous years
* Evaluate customer and product performance
* Track forecast accuracy and supply-chain metrics
* Analyze regional and market-level performance
* Identify key business trends and opportunities

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Views

### 🏠 Home

A centralized landing page providing navigation to all major business views:

* Finance View
* Sales View
* Marketing View
* Supply Chain View
* Executive View
* Sales Trend
* P&L Check
* Support

### 💰 Finance View

Provides detailed financial analysis through:

* Net Sales
* Gross Margin %
* Net Profit %
* Profit & Loss Statement
* Gross Sales
* Discounts and deductions
* COGS
* Operating expenses
* Net Profit
* Performance comparison with benchmarks and previous years

### 🤝 Sales View

Analyzes sales performance across customers and products, including:

* Customer Performance
* Product Performance
* Net Sales
* Gross Margin
* Gross Margin %
* Performance Matrix
* Top and bottom customers/products
* Unit Economics

### 📢 Marketing View

Provides insights into marketing and profitability performance through:

* Product-level profitability
* Region/Market/Customer performance
* Net Sales
* Gross Margin
* Net Profit
* Market performance
* Customer and product comparisons
* Unit Economics

### 🚚 Supply Chain View

Focuses on forecasting and inventory-related performance:

* Forecast Accuracy
* Net Error
* Absolute Error
* Forecast Accuracy trends
* Customer-level metrics
* Product-level metrics
* Risk classification
* Out-of-stock and excess-inventory indicators

### 👔 Executive View

Provides a high-level management dashboard covering:

* Revenue by Division
* Revenue by Channel
* Yearly Revenue Trends
* Gross Margin %
* Net Profit %
* Market Share
* Top Customers
* Top Products
* Regional/Sub-zone performance
* Competitive market analysis

### 📈 Sales Trend

Tracks changes in:

* Net Sales
* Gross Margin %
* Monthly performance
* Year-over-year trends

### ✅ P&L Check

A validation view used to compare financial calculations and verify the consistency of key P&L metrics across different years.

## 🚀 Outcome

This project demonstrates how **Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and data modeling** can be combined to convert complex business data into an intuitive and interactive **360° business intelligence solution**.

It provides a single reporting environment where different business functions can analyze their performance and make informed, data-driven decisions.
